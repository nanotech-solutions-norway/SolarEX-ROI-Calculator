Pathway logic

Quartz and Titan are different technical mechanisms.

SolarEX Quartz — SiO₂

Passive, UV-independent SiO₂ surface architecture for hydrophobic and oleophobic easy-clean behavior. Best aligned with dust, pollen, mineral soiling, high-latitude regions and low-UV conditions.

  • No UV activation required
  • Reference Europe uplift: 10%
  • Reference Middle East uplift: 2%

SolarEX Titan — TiO₂

Active TiO₂ photocatalytic pathway for UV-supported organic-contaminant decomposition and hydrophilic rinse behavior. Best aligned with UV-sufficient sites and organic, biological or industrial atmospheric contamination.

  • UV activation required
  • 360-day study uplift: 5.15%
  • Site UV availability is a key selection variable
